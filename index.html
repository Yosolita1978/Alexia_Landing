Questions

Do you create meal plans?

I focus on meal ideas and flexible frameworks rather than strict plans—so changes fit your real life.

No. This approach is minimal-tracking and centered on well-being, not perfection.

Primarily in-home in Gig Harbor and Tacoma, with select sessions at grocery stores or other agreed locations.

Do you offer virtual sessions?

Not at this time—my focus is on in-person, hands-on support.
